# Model and supplied results

The exact verified forward_model.py is unchanged (SHA256 b9593cc2cd1c08b5b9fe0ce8293b2f779a56c2511b6ef139c59a65b939483fa8). States S/E/I/R/D are persons. Rates/beta/amplitudes are day^-1. Simultaneous explicit Euler timestep is 0.1 day, through day 199.9. The transmission denominator is total S+E+I+R+D. Initial state is [N-E0-I0,E0,I0,0,0]; E0 and I0 come from each posterior row, not observations or optimizer guesses. No state reset or held-out assimilation occurs.

beta(t)=beta0+sum_j A_j/[1+exp((location_j-t)/width)], width=1 day. Fixed gamma_e=1/15, gamma_i=1/5, gamma_r=1/11, gamma_d=1/750. Equations and normalized Gaussian likelihood are in forward_model.py. Observation SD=sigma I(t), sigma=0.1. Inference used daily observations 0–159 including day zero; forecast observations are 160–199. All numeric settings, populations, locations, prior supports and separate ML bounds are supplied per configuration.

Independent signed Uniform priors have magnitude 0.5; automatic signs were selected by joint training ML. E0/I0 prior upper bounds are 0.001N, as tabulated. Inference was conditional on locations and signs, excluding their selection uncertainty. ML bounds and priors are not confidence intervals. Manual configurations are retrospective; case-one manual centers coincide with its generating centers. Generating curves are supplied as synthetic truth; case two has no true coefficients or locations for this fitted sum-of-sigmoids basis.

Historical sampling used original repository worker initialization (duplicate initial RNG states possible). Case two used fixed 50 attempted moves per stage; other supplied cases used original adaptive 5–10 controls. Provenance/seed/source hashes are in sampling_provenance.json. No sampler is executed or included. Numerical reproduction is not evidence of posterior convergence.

The historical target rejected beta < -1e-12 on 0:0.1:159.9 and required positive daily I/finite likelihood. It did not enforce forecast validity or every compartment in the target. Native-grid postchecks through 199.9 require finite states, beta >= -1e-12, each compartment >= -1e-8 persons and conservation error <=1e-6 persons. No clipping or repairs.

Ottawa retains all 4,000 original rows and original weights. Its existing exclusion_mask.npy excludes original zero-based indices 443,2837,3048,3113 only from summaries. Both fit and forecast summaries use the same 3,996 rows with equal normalized weights. This is the saved posterior conditioned on physical validity over the evaluated training and forecast domain; 4 of 4,000 samples excluded. The original mask is checked for the exact supplied parameter file, model, data and horizon before use. It is never applied automatically to alternative samples. New positive-weight invalid trajectories are retained and reported; plots/summaries are withheld. No experimental day-156 configuration is included.
